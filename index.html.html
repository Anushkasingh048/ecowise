Honest data policy
The starting products and ratings are demonstration examples, not verified environmental measurements. The score is a transparent screening aid, not a scientific lifecycle assessment. Check sources before relying on a claim.

How to interpret the results

Cost per use

Purchase price ÷ expected total uses. This excludes repairs, refills, replacement parts and disposal costs unless you add them yourself.

Transparent screening score

The score is a weighted average of durability (40%), end-of-life options (35%) and packaging (25%). Each rating is 0–5, converted to 0–100. Zero means unknown and is excluded from the average, so incomplete data cannot be mistaken for a confirmed poor rating. The score is still a user-entered screening estimate, not a carbon footprint.

Important trade-offs

A reusable item may need many uses to offset its manufacturing impact. Results also depend on actual lifespan, production, transport, cleaning, local recycling facilities and what product it replaces.
